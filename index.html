THE PROCESS

Three steps, one roll at a time

No queues, no automated minilabs. A real person handles your film from envelope to scan — whether it's a roll of city landmarks or your kid's birthday party.

01 / MAIL

Drop it in the mail

Pack your exposed roll in any padded envelope and send it to our lab. A prepaid label is included with every order.

02 / DEVELOP

We develop by hand

Each roll is processed in small batches with fresh chemistry, monitored by temperature and time — not a conveyor belt.

03 / SCAN & RETURN

Scans land in your inbox

High-resolution scans arrive within 48 hours of processing. Negatives and prints follow by mail a few days later.
